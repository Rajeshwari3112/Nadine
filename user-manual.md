**Facility Condition Assessment (FCA) Basics**

* Sound Property Management involves regular checks of the building’s health.
* Maintenance needs increase with a building’s age and lifecycle renewals become necessary.
* FCA proactively assesses property compliance with:
  * Building Codes
  * Accessibility Regulations
  * Fire/Life Safety Codes
* Environmental and Energy Sustainability Requirements
* FCA provides renewal budgets and timeframes with scientific evidence and justifications.
* Accurate FCA data supports strategic decision making and renewal planning.
* FCA helps in scientific prioritization of renewals across the portfolio.
* FCA helps in optimized funding allocation across the portfolio.
* FCA is a great tool when assessing the condition of buildings or in maintaining them in top shape.

### **System Rating** <a href="#system-rating" id="system-rating"></a>

**Rating**

**Definition**

Excellent

System new installed, or comes in the first quarter of its useful life and just need regular maintenance. Recommend replacement end of service life if it is within the horizon of the report.

Good

System is in the second quarter of its useful life, responds well to its function, its performance / requires minor repairs. Recommend replacement end of service life.

Average

System is in the third quarter of its useful life, responds well to its function, its performance / requires minor repairs. Recommend replacement end of service life.

Fair

System is in the fourth quarter of its useful life, and requires some repairs or replacement of some parts. Recommend replacement end of service life ( it may need to increase service life if the remaining is less than 3 years and system in working condition).

Poor

System's useful life is end or exceeded / requires major repairs or frequent repairs occurred. Recommend replacement.

Defective

System's service life is largely exceeded / requires many repairs or replacement and malfunction. Recommend replacement immediately.

### **Hierarchical Setup** <a href="#hierarchical-setup" id="hierarchical-setup"></a>

**Parent-Child Hierarchy**The Application is based on a parent-child hierarchy defined in the following order for all new FCA’s: Facility Condition Assessment (FCA) year completed, Portfolio, Site and Asset; as presented below.

![](https://test-manual-doc.gitbook.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fstagedcc.nadinebca.com%2Fapp%2Fpublic%2Fuser_manual%2Fen%2Fdrex_hierarchical_setup_custom.png\&width=300\&dpr=4\&quality=100\&sign=33248acf\&sv=2)

The hierarchy is used as a naming convention in addition to the Uniformat number to identify an asset, system or requirement.The Site and Asset Codes are short forms of the names.
